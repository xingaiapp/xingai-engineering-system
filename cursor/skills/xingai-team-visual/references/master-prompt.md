```text
Create a premium illustrated team portrait for XingAI.

STYLE:
Use a polished Chinese-inspired modern cartoon / anime illustration style.
Hand-painted digital illustration with clean ink outlines, expressive faces,
soft watercolor brush textures, subtle paper texture, warm highlights,
faint ink-wash mountains and pavilions in the background,
high-quality character rendering, playful but professional startup branding.

The visual language should feel:
- Smart
- Friendly
- Humorous
- Premium
- Memorable
- Slightly Chinese fantasy inspired
- Modern AI startup
- Not childish
- Not photorealistic

CHARACTER SYSTEM:

星哥 — Founder / Boss
Position: top center, visually dominant, seated on a carved wooden chair.
Personality: calm, confident, clever, slightly mysterious.
Appearance: Chinese male, mature, dark hair with some gray,
friendly smile, wearing a dark XingAI hoodie.
Signature props: golden crown and XingAI coffee mug.
Role: 愿景 · 战略 · 最终决策 (Vision / Strategy / Final Decision).
Color: gold / warm yellow.

THE FIVE LEADERS (one row below 星哥, left to right):

1. 至尊宝 — 群主 | QA总闸 | 星哥缺席时拍板
Personality: confident, humorous, decisive.
Appearance: Monkey King inspired young man, messy dark hair tied up,
golden circlet headband, red scarf, staff on his back, thumbs-up, confident grin.
System: 质量保证体系 (QA) — Quality Gatekeeper.
Color: deep blue.

2. 牛夫人 — 内容策略 | 审改 | 独立复核
Personality: strong, decisive, demanding.
Appearance: elegant woman in dark traditional dress, dark hair in an updo
with gold ornaments and tassels, stern expression, pointing at the viewer.
System: 内容策略体系 — Content Strategy.
Color: purple.

3. 小甜甜 — 发布 | 养号 | 送审
Personality: warm, playful, charming.
Appearance: young woman, long dark hair with pink flower ornaments,
pink traditional outfit, chin resting on hands, winking, small hearts.
System: 发布与增长体系 — Publish & Growth.
Color: pink.

4. 二当家 — 复核 | 重剪 | 核验
Personality: humorous, observant, resourceful.
Appearance: wuxia-style man, dark cap with white headband, stubble and mustache,
gray traditional robe, gourd at his side, laughing, pointing up.
System: 复核与优化体系 — Recheck & Edit.
Color: green.

5. 华安（唐伯虎） — 代码 | 技术 | 工具
Personality: clever, cheerful, scholarly.
Appearance: young scholar, black scholar's hat with white headband,
white-and-gray robe with dark trim, holding scrolls / bamboo slips,
bamboo behind him, friendly smile.
System: 技术与工具体系 — Tech & Tools.
Color: sky blue (lighter than 至尊宝's deep blue).

TEAM STRUCTURE:

星哥 is the central leader.
Draw a hand-painted arrow from 星哥 down to each of the five leaders.
Do not add extra relationship lines between the leaders.

COMPOSITION:

                      星哥
                 Founder / Boss

  至尊宝    牛夫人    小甜甜    二当家    华安

        五位负责人 · 一个团队 · 一个使命
        Five Leaders · One Team · One Mission

Each leader has a colored brush-stroke nameplate with the Chinese name
and one short line under it. Use ONE nameplate set for the whole image:

Personality set (default for posters / About page):
星哥 神秘大Boss · 至尊宝 嘴最硬 · 牛夫人 最会追责 · 小甜甜 最会哄人 ·
二当家 背锅侠 + 情报员 · 华安 最会写码

Org-role set (for org charts): the role lines listed above with each character.
Optionally one small icon row per leader:
QA shield, content edit, paper-plane publish, film recheck, code / gear tools.

BRAND:

Subtly integrate XingAI branding:
"XingAI — AI for a Smarter, Happier Life" in a corner.
Optional handwritten note: "Better Decisions, Brighter Days".
Do not make the logo dominate the illustration.

BACKGROUND:

White or very light warm background.
Watercolor brush circle behind each character in that character's color.
Keep generous white space.

QUALITY:

High-end editorial illustration.
Detailed faces.
Consistent character proportions.
Consistent visual identity across all six characters.
Professional enough for a startup website's About / Team section.

ASPECT RATIO:

16:9 landscape.

IMPORTANT:

Exactly six characters: 星哥 and the five leaders.
Do not create random additional people.
Do not change the character identities.
Do not make the characters photorealistic.
Do not use generic corporate stock-photo aesthetics.
Do not overcrowd the background.
Do not change the hierarchy.

The final image should immediately communicate:

"星哥 and five very different leaders, one team building XingAI."
```
